# 🚬 Tobacco Shop POS & Inventory Management System

A specialized, feature-rich point-of-sale (POS) and accounting software designed specifically for tobacco and retail shops. Manage your inventory, track multiple card readers (POS terminals), handle discounts, and scan barcodes seamlessly—all in one place.

---

## ✨ Key Features

*   **📷 Barcode Scanner Integration:** Quickly scan product barcodes using the device camera for fast sales and inventory checks.
*   **📦 Inventory Management:** Dedicated stock control with a comprehensive goods-receiving form (Warehouse Entry) to keep your stock updated.
*   **💳 Multi-POS Tracking:** Accurately record and track which card reader machine/POS terminal each payment was processed through.
*   **💰 Customer Balances & Discounts:** Maintain customer account balances and apply flexible discounts during checkout.
*   **📊 Comprehensive Reporting:** Generate detailed financial reports, sales summaries, and balance sheets.
*   **💼 Tailored Accounting:** Specifically structured to handle the day-to-day financial workflow of a tobacco and retail store.
